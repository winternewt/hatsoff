I am assessing the novelty of a tight-binding study of the **hat aperiodic
monotile**: E=0 zero modes of its (non-bipartite) vertex adjacency graph under
random **site dilution**, analyzed with the **Gallai–Edmonds** maximum-matching
structure theorem (adjacency nullity vs matching deficiency def = N−2ν). Key
numerical findings: (#1) the gap (nullity − deficiency) is extensive and grows
with system size, peaking near dilution p≈0.08; (#2) trapped zero-mode weight
fraction is intensive (0→0.44); (#3) the largest factor-critical ("blossom")
component grows with size, peaking at small p≈0.03; (#4) the zero-mode *support*
spatially spans only near the clean limit, with a finite-size trend suggesting
the spanning threshold **p_c → 0** (any finite dilution localizes it as
L→∞) — in contrast to the **finite** p_c on diluted bipartite and periodic
non-bipartite lattices.
